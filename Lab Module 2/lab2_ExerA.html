About the Event

Faculty Technology Day is a one-day event organised to introduce students to programming, technology and career opportunities. Students can attend talks, participate in workshops and meet technology professionals.

Guest Speaker: Introduction to Programming

Speaker: Dr. Muhammad Az'am. Learn the fundamentals of programming and discover how software is developed to solve real-world problems.

Activity: Web Development Workshop

Join our hands-on workshop to learn how to create a simple website using HTML. Participants will build their first webpage with guidance from the instructors.
