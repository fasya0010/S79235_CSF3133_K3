About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu Food Festival is an exciting event that celebrates the traditional food and rich culinary heritage of Terengganu. Visitors can enjoy a variety of delicious local dishes prepared by local food vendors.

The festival provides an opportunity for visitors to experience the unique flavours of Terengganu while supporting local businesses and food entrepreneurs. Families and food lovers are welcome to join this special celebration.

Free Entry! Everyone is welcome to join the festival.
