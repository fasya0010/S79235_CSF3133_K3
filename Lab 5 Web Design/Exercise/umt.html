About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It started as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Putra Malaysia in 1979. The centre provided training facilities for students and research facilities for lecturers.

In June 1996, the institution was renamed Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). Later, it became Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) and was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was upgraded to a full-fledged university and officially became Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT). Today, UMT focuses on education and research in marine science, environmental studies, technology and other academic fields.
